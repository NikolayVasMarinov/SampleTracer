The idea

Hip hop is built on other records. SampleTracer keeps that lineage in one place: who produced a track, and exactly which source records it samples. Open a track to see its samples, open a record to see every track that flipped it.

The three things you track

Producers
The person behind the beat: country, active-since year and, for US producers, the region they come from.
Source records
The original songs that get sampled, with artist, release year, genre and length.
Tracks
The hip hop tracks themselves: tempo, style, key and length, linked to one producer and to any number of source records.

Good to know

  • A track can’t sample a record that was released after the track itself.
  • A producer who still has tracks can’t be deleted; remove or move those tracks first.
  • Lengths are typed as m:ss (for example 3:45) and shown the same way.
  • The Most sampled page ranks source records by how many tracks use them.
